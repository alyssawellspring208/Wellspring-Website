About WellSpring

One coordinated plan for long-term health, coming soon to Pensacola and Birmingham.

Our mission

Healthier, stronger, more vibrant at every stage

At WellSpring, our mission is to empower individuals to live healthier, stronger, and more vibrant lives through every stage of adulthood. We provide personalized, evidence-based care that brings together hormonal health, metabolic wellness, nutrition, fitness, prevention, and healthy aging.

We serve adults 35 to 60 and up who feel their body isn't responding the way it used to. Our care focuses on women's health, and we welcome men too.

Our leadership

The people behind WellSpring

RB

Randy Beckham

Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer

Randy Beckham has spent more than 25 years as a C-level executive and venture builder in healthcare, pharmacy benefit management, sports performance, and medical devices, taking multiple startups from launch to more than $100 million in annual revenue.

He founded WellSpring to close the midlife health gap and move care from treating symptoms after they appear to protecting healthspan for life. WellSpring is a family-focused company rooted in Alabama and Florida.

DR

Dr. Don Reiff, MD, FACS

Chief Medical Officer

Dr. Reiff is a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons. He oversees urgent care at UAB in Birmingham, serves as medical director for an Alabama sheriff's department, and has held several military clinical roles.

LB

Layla Beverly, ACNP-BC

Director of Clinical Operations

Layla Amiri Beverly is a board-certified nurse practitioner with more than 24 years in nursing, nearly 20 in advanced practice. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing in 2001 and her Master of Science in Nursing in 2007, both from the University of Alabama in Huntsville.

Her clinical work spans medical wellness, weight management, hormone optimization, IV therapy, aesthetics, oncology, sleep medicine, and pain management.
