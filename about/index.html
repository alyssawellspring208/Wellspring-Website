About WellSpring

One coordinated plan for long-term health, coming soon to Pensacola and Birmingham.

Our mission

Care for every stage of adulthood

WellSpring's mission is to help adults improve their health at every stage of adulthood. Our personalized, evidence-based care brings hormonal health, metabolic wellness, nutrition, fitness, prevention, and healthy aging into one plan.

We serve adults 35 to 60 and up who notice changes in how their body responds. Our care focuses on women's health, and we welcome men too.

Leadership team

Meet Our Leadership Team

These are the leaders guiding WellSpring today. Our wider clinical and advisory team is expanding as the clinics develop.

RB

Randy Beckham

Strategy | Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer

Randy Beckham has spent more than 25 years as a C-level executive and venture builder in healthcare, pharmacy benefit management, sports performance, and medical devices, taking multiple startups from launch to more than $100 million in annual revenue.

He founded WellSpring to close the midlife health gap with care that protects healthspan before symptoms appear. WellSpring is a family-focused company rooted in Alabama and Florida.

Chris Cutchens, CPA

Finance | Chief Financial Officer

Chris Cutchens brings more than 20 years of experience in financial leadership, accounting, and mergers and acquisitions. At WellSpring, he oversees financial strategy and supports the systems needed for long-term operations and growth.

Chris has served in senior finance and operations roles at MidAmerica Administrative & Retirement Solutions and Aspire Financial Services, with earlier leadership experience at Carrier Enterprise, Watsco, MarineMax, and KPMG. He holds degrees in accounting and accounting information systems from the University of South Florida and is a licensed CPA in Florida.

LB

Layla Beverly, ACNP-BC

Clinical Operations | Director of Clinical Operations

Layla Amiri Beverly is a board-certified nurse practitioner with more than 24 years in nursing, nearly 20 in advanced practice. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing in 2001 and her Master of Science in Nursing in 2007, both from the University of Alabama in Huntsville.

Her clinical work spans medical wellness, weight management, hormone optimization, IV therapy, aesthetics, oncology, sleep medicine, and pain management.

WellSpring is developing a broader team to support our future clinical services. Additional team members will be announced as roles are confirmed.
