Services

One coordinated plan across three layers of care. Every plan starts with an evaluation by a licensed clinician.

Layer one

Optimize

Core medical wellness

  • Bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (BHRT)

    Customized hormone optimization for women and men, prescribed and monitored by licensed clinicians.

  • Medical weight management (GLP-1)

    Clinician-guided weight loss with metabolic monitoring and lean muscle preservation.

  • Peptide therapy

    Peptide protocols prescribed when clinically appropriate.

  • Metabolic and diagnostic optimization

    Comprehensive blood panels and a baseline biomarker review.

Layer two

Restore

Vitality and cellular wellness

  • IV hydration and micronutrient therapy

    Custom vitamin and mineral IV infusions for hydration.

  • NAD+ injections and infusions

    NAD+ therapy by injection or IV under clinical supervision.

  • Injectable wellness and recovery

    IM injections and recovery protocols with ongoing biomarker tracking.

Layer three

Renew

Aesthetics and healthy aging

  • Regenerative aesthetics and PRP

    Platelet-rich plasma treatments that use your own growth factors for skin rejuvenation.

    IPL photofacials, RF microneedling, skin tightening, and body contouring.

  • Injectables and clinical skincare

    Wrinkle relaxers, fillers, medical-grade facials, and clinical skincare lines.
